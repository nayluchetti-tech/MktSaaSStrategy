# NRB Landing Page Analysis & Recommendations

**URL:** events.pray.com/book-your-nrb-meeting-with-pray.com-booth-401-nashville

---

## Current Page Structure

1. Header: PRAY.COM | NRB logos + "Book Your Meeting" CTA
2. Hero: "Modern Tools. Timeless Truth. Maximum Reach."
3. Form intro: "Book Your Meeting with PRAY.COM"
4. Form fields: Name, Email, Phone, Ministry URL, Distribution channels
5. CTA: "Reserve Your Time Slot"
6. Social proof: "Trusted by Ministry Pioneers" (4 photos)
7. Products: "Game-Changing Tools. One Booth Visit." (3 products)
8. Bottom CTA: "Book a Funnel Strategy Call Today"
9. Trust stats: "18 Million Christians" + 4.8 stars / 140K reviews
10. Footer

---

## What's Working Well

| Element | Why It Works |
|---------|--------------|
| Clean dark design | Aligns with brand, professional, premium feel |
| Ministry pioneers section | Recognizable names build credibility |
| Qualifying question | Helps route leads, shows you understand their world |
| Progress indicator | Reduces form anxiety |
| Trust stats at bottom | 18M users + 4.8 stars is compelling |
| Gold accent color | Premium feel, draws eye to CTAs |

## Recommended Improvements

### 1. HERO SECTION

**Current:**
> "Modern Tools. Timeless Truth. Maximum Reach."

**Problem:** Generic, doesn't speak to specific pain or outcome.

**Recommended Options:**

| Option | Headline |
|--------|----------|
| Problem-led | **"Struggling to Grow Your Ministry's Reach?"** |
| Outcome-led | **"3× Your Reach. ¼ the Cost. Prove the Impact."** |
| Event-specific | **"NRB 2026: The Tools Your Ministry Has Been Waiting For"** |
| Direct | **"We Solve Ministry Growth Problems."** |

**Add a subheadline** (currently missing):
> "See how PRAY.COM helps ministries reach more people, disciple deeper, and grow sustainably."

---

### 2. FORM SECTION INTRO

**Current:**
> "Book Your Meeting with PRAY.COM"
> "Share a little about your ministry, and we'll connect you with the appropriate person later today, tomorrow, or after NRB."

**Problem:** Doesn't communicate value. Why should they book?

**Recommended:**

**Title:** "Book Your Meeting with PRAY.COM"

**Subtext Options:**
- "In 15 minutes, discover how to reach 3× more people at ¼ the cost—with tools built for ministry."
- "Get a personalized walkthrough of the platform trusted by 18 million Christians."
- "Limited NRB meeting slots available. Reserve yours now."

---

### 3. ADD URGENCY (Event-Specific)

**Missing element:** No urgency or scarcity for NRB-specific timing.

**Add near form:**
- "Only [X] meeting slots remaining for NRB"
- "Booth 401 · February 17-20 · Nashville"
- "Book now—slots fill fast during NRB week"

---

### 4. SOCIAL PROOF PLACEMENT

**Current:** Ministry pioneers section appears AFTER the form.

**Recommendation:** Move "Trusted by Ministry Pioneers" ABOVE or beside the form. People need trust signals before giving contact info.

**Also add:** A short quote from one of the featured leaders.
> "PRAY.COM has transformed how we reach and disciple our audience." — Jack Graham, Power Point Ministries

---

### 5. PRODUCTS SECTION

**Current Title:**
> "Game-Changing Tools. One Booth Visit."
> "PRAY.COM is reshaping ministry in 2026. Here's what you need to know:"

**Problem:** "Game-changing" is overused. Second line is vague.

**Recommended Title Options:**
- **"Three Tools. One Growth Platform."**
- **"Solve Your Biggest Growth Challenges"**
- **"Built for Ministry Growth"**

**Recommended Subhead:**
> "Whether you need more reach, deeper engagement, or sustainable funding—we've got you covered."

---

### 7. BOTTOM CTA SECTION

**Current:**
> "Book a Funnel Strategy Call Today:"
> [Reserve Your VIP Meeting Slot →]

**Problem:** "Funnel Strategy Call" is too narrow—implies only SuperFunnel.

**Recommended:**

**Headline Options:**
- "Ready to Solve Your Growth Problem?"
- "See PRAY.COM in Action"
- "Let's Talk About Your Ministry's Growth"

**CTA Options:**
- "Reserve Your Meeting Slot →"
- "Book Your NRB Meeting →"
- "Claim Your Spot at Booth 401 →"

---

### 8. MISSING ELEMENTS TO ADD

| Element | Where to Add | Why |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| **Specific stats** (3× reach, ¼ cost) | Hero or above form | Concrete proof beats vague claims |
| **"What to expect"** section | Near form | Reduces meeting anxiety |
| **Booth number prominently** | Hero or sticky header | Helps people find you at NRB |
| **Testimonial quote** | Near ministry pioneers | Adds depth to social proof |
| **Ministry logos** | Below pioneers | Visual credibility |

**"What to Expect" Example:**
> **In your 15-minute meeting, you'll:**
> - See a personalized demo based on your ministry's needs
> - Get a custom growth assessment
> - Learn which tools fit your budget and goals

---

### 9. CTA BUTTON COPY

**Current CTAs:**
- Header: "Book Your Meeting"
- Form: "Reserve Your Time Slot"
- Bottom: "Reserve Your VIP Meeting Slot →"

**Inconsistency:** Three different CTAs. Pick one and use consistently.

**Recommended Primary CTA:** "Reserve Your Meeting Slot"

**Alternative options:**
- "Book Your NRB Meeting"
- "Claim Your Spot"
- "Get Your Demo"
